# DEC-002: AI output is untrusted and non-authoritative

- **Status:** Superseded by DEC-011 on 2026-09-19
- **Decision:** AI was originally permitted to propose source, explanations, assumptions, capabilities, and tests, while remaining unable to validate, approve, activate, publish, or control outputs.
- **Consequences:** The firmware-proposal portion of this decision is no longer part of project scope. DEC-011 retains and tightens the non-authority boundary for the sole remaining AI function.
- **Revisit when:** Historical record only; use DEC-011 for current requirements.
