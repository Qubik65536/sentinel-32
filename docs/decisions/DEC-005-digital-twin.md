# DEC-005: Deterministic software launch-pad twin

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Demonstrate with a schema-defined rocket ground-launch digital twin and normalized values; require no external electronics or physical control.
- **Consequences:** Faults and timelines are repeatable and safe to demonstrate. Generic scenario machinery cannot hard-code rocket identifiers. Results make no claim about real launch vehicle physics or operating limits.
- **Revisit when:** The generic engine is validated and a new simulation adds value without implying physical authority.
